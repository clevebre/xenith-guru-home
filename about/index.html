About

Xenith Guru is made by brooke.ltd, in Belfast. It is a place to learn about AI in plain words, at your own pace.

The lessons are written in brooke.ltd's house voice with the help of AI, and checked against the sources each lesson names before they are published. Each lesson says when it was last reviewed.

The site sets no cookies, runs no tracking, has no accounts or forms, and loads nothing from anywhere else.
