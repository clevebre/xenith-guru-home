Glossary

The words you will meet, in plain English.

AI
Artificial intelligence: computer systems that do tasks we usually think need human intelligence, such as writing, recognising images or answering questions.
Generative AI
AI that produces new text, images, sound or video in response to what you ask.
Large language model (LLM)
A program trained on a vast amount of text to predict likely next words. Chat assistants are built on them.
Chat assistant
A tool you talk to in plain language, built on a large language model.
Model
The trained program itself, as distinct from the app or website you use it through.
Provider
The company or organisation that runs the model or the service.
Prompt
What you type or say to the assistant: your question or instruction.
Context
Everything the assistant can see while it answers: your prompt, the conversation so far, and any documents you have given it.
Context window
The limit on how much text an assistant can take into account at once. Very long conversations or documents may be partly dropped.
Token
A small piece of text, often part of a word, that models read and write in. Limits and prices are often counted in tokens.
Training data
The text, images or other material a model learned its patterns from.
Training cut-off
The date after which a model learned nothing new. It may not know about later events unless it is connected to search.
Hallucination
An answer that sounds right but is false or made up, such as an invented book or a wrong date.
Source
Where a claim comes from. A source only counts once you have opened it and found the claim in it.
Bias
A tilt in answers that comes from patterns in the training data or the way a system was built, and can treat some people or views unfairly.
Local model
A model that runs on your own device, so what you type does not leave it.
Privacy notice
The provider's statement of what it does with what you type and with your account.
Agent
An AI system that takes actions as well as answering, such as searching, filling in forms or running steps for you. Its actions need the same checking as its words.
