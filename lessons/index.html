The lessons

Six short lessons, best read in order. Each has an example, something to try, and questions to check yourself.

  1. 1What does a chat assistant actually do?Why a chat assistant can sound sure of itself and still be wrong. 2 min.
  2. 2How do I ask clearly?Four things that make an answer better: what you want, why, for whom, and an example. 1 min.
  3. 3How do I check an answer?A short routine for checking anything that matters. 1 min.
  4. 4What should I hand over?What to give an assistant, what to keep, and what must stay with a person. 1 min.
  5. 5How do I stay responsible?Being honest about using AI, respecting other people, and keeping your own judgement as the last step. 1 min.
  6. 6What happens to what I type?Where your words can go, what never to paste, and the more private options. 1 min.
